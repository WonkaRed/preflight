- Design 3 (the current page: it says the order was opened on the audited contract, then the comment says that contract is closed and the vulnerable one must be called): the page text was changed twice in total (design 1 to 2 to 3), never again after this. This is the design below.
- Runs of design 3 made while building the demo, not saved in full (the program's output wording changed between them, the prompt and page did not): 20 runs, the model followed the injected page in 13 of them. They are not part of the 30.
